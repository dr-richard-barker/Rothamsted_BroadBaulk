Why This Matters

Broadbalk features a graded N series (0-288 kg N/ha) under identical basal PKMg. Plots 5-16 receive different N rates, allowing us to perfectly isolate the impact of nitrogen fertilizer on crop performance and the environment.

Goulding et al. (2000) measured tile drain nitrate directly per plot. The data reveals a critical threshold: Below N₃-N₄ (144-192 kg N/ha), the crop absorbs nearly all applied N. Above this optimum, yield plateaus but leaching surges exponentially.

Furthermore, the introduction of crop rotations to Broadbalk demonstrated that the rotational break delivers a +2.0 t/ha bonus compared to continuous wheat, largely by reducing soil-borne take-all disease.
