Why This Matters ▼

Broadbalk intentionally updates cultivars to reflect British farming practice. In 1968, the field was redesigned: traditional long-straw varieties were replaced with semi-dwarf cultivars carrying Rht dwarfing genes.

Austin et al. (1980) showed total biomass hadn't changed — yield gain came entirely from harvest index. Mackay et al. (2011) showed 88% of post-1982 yield gain was genetic.
