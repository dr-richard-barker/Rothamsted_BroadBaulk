Why This Matters

This dataset calibrated and validated the RothC model — the benchmark for IPCC global soil carbon modeling (Jenkinson, 1990). Poulton et al. (2018) showed the COP21 '4 per 1000' target is physically achievable short-term but impossible to sustain. Soils are NOT infinite carbon sinks.

Reality Check

Applying 35 t/ha FYM annually is completely unscalable globally — there isn't enough manure — and causes extreme nitrate leaching.

4 per 1000 Reality Check

Comparing target annual rate (4‰) vs. actual rates achieved on Broadbalk.

The "4 per 1000" initiative, launched at COP21, aims to increase global soil organic carbon stocks by 0.4% per year. However, Broadbalk data shows that while high rates can be achieved initially with massive inputs, they decay as soils reach a new equilibrium.

  • Initial burst: FYM first 20 years reached ~18-43‰.
  • Diminishing returns: By 60 years, it fell to ~5‰.
  • Saturation: At 120 years, it slowed to ~1‰.
