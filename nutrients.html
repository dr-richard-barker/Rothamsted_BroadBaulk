Why This Matters

The Rothamsted Sample Archive contains 300,000+ sealed jars from 1843. Fan et al. (2008) analyzed these and found grain minerals were stable for 120 years, then collapsed exactly when semi-dwarf cultivars were introduced in 1968. Root uptake couldn't match rapid starch accumulation. Selenium followed a different pattern driven by SO₂ emissions.
