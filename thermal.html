Why This Matters ▼

Rothamsted hosts the longest continuous daily met record in the UK (since 1853). Warming has been ~1.2°C. For winter wheat, heat stress around anthesis (flowering, late May-mid June) and during grain filling (June-July) is the most dangerous climatic bottleneck.

Temperatures exceeding 25-27°C cause pollen sterility. Warmer temperatures also shorten the grain-filling duration, leading to smaller, lighter grains and significantly reducing overall yield.
